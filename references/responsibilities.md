# Responsibility menus (onboarding question 2)

Used to pin down what the person wants to do all day, not just a title. Show only the menu for the target role(s) they confirmed. Ask them to pick up to 4 "want" and up to 3 "avoid". Titles lie; responsibilities are what the posting text gets matched against.

These menus are working hypotheses from general hiring practice, not validated against hiring-manager data. If a posting contradicts a menu, trust the posting.

## GTM Engineering
Want: build outbound/enrichment workflows (Clay, n8n, Make, Zapier) / write scripts and API integrations / build and maintain lead-scoring and routing / design AI agents for prospecting or research / own the GTM data model and tool stack / run experiments on pipeline conversion
Avoid candidates: carrying a sales quota / pure SDR volume work / on-call platform engineering

## AI Product Management
Want: own roadmap for an AI feature or platform / write specs and evals / work with research and engineering on model behavior / define success metrics and pricing / run customer discovery on AI use cases
Avoid candidates: pure project management / pure growth experimentation

## Product Marketing
Want: positioning and messaging / launches and GTM plans / sales enablement and battlecards / competitive and customer research / pricing and packaging input / analyst and press relations
Avoid candidates: demand-gen channel operations / content production at volume

## RevOps
Want: CRM architecture (Salesforce, HubSpot) / pipeline and forecasting reporting / territory, quota and comp plan design / process and handoff design / data hygiene and tooling stack
Avoid candidates: day-to-day admin tickets only / pure finance modelling

## Forward-Deployed Engineer
Want: customer-facing implementations / writing integrations in the customer's environment / prototyping for deals / travel or on-site work / turning field learnings into product feedback
Avoid candidates: heavy travel / pure support / long-running platform roadmap work

## Marketing Engineering
Want: build marketing infrastructure and tracking / site, CMS and web performance / attribution and data pipelines / automation across the martech stack / internal tools for marketers / experimentation platforms
Avoid candidates: campaign execution / copywriting / paid media management

## AEO/GEO
Want: AI-search visibility strategy (ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews) / content and schema optimization for answer engines / citation and share-of-voice measurement / technical SEO foundations / experiments on retrieval and ranking behavior
Avoid candidates: traditional link building only / pure content writing

## AI Transformation
Want: identify and prioritize AI use cases inside a business / roll out AI tools to teams / change management and training / build internal agents and workflows / measure adoption and ROI / advise executives
Avoid candidates: pure strategy decks without building / pure IT administration

## Capturing the answer
Write the picks to the profile card as `Want:` and `Avoid:` lines. In ranking, a posting whose duties match an "avoid" item is dropped or flagged; matches on "want" items raise fit. If a menu does not fit the person, take their own words instead.
